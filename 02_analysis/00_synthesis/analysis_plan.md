# Analysis Plan

## Objective

Turn the Whiteman Airport case into a source-backed systems transformation framework suitable for strategic planning, graduate capstone work, or a decision-support report.

## Workstream Sequence

1. Source validation and timeline
2. Stakeholder architecture
3. FAA/legal constraints
4. Mission decomposition
5. Operational dependencies
6. Public health and environmental justice
7. Economic and land-use analysis
8. Safety and incident timeline
9. Risk matrix
10. Alternatives analysis
11. Transition roadmap
12. Governance model
13. PMO/dashboard structure
14. Digital twin concept
15. Phased acquisition strategy

## Key Analytical Questions

- What decisions are actually available to LA County under FAA obligations?
- Which alternatives are legally feasible, operationally feasible, fiscally feasible, and politically legitimate?
- What would be lost, shifted, or created under each alternative?
- What must happen before any transition decision can be responsibly made?
- What data system or digital twin would improve the quality of public decision-making?

## Near-Term Deliverables

- Source-backed timeline
- Stakeholder register
- FAA/legal constraints memo
- Mission decomposition map
- Initial risk register
- Alternatives matrix
