## Critical Summary of Five Best Website Additions

### 1. Whiteman Airport Land Use Study

Source ID: SRC-002

This is the central official project website and should anchor the report's description of what the County is actually studying. Its strongest value is that it defines the study scope in the County-facing language: existing conditions, closure process, closure implications, stakeholder feedback, preferred options, and economic impact analysis across land-use options.

Critical use: cite this source to establish the formal study frame, but do not treat it as an independent evaluation. It is a public-facing process site, not the final analytical report. Its main limitation is that it summarizes the study rather than providing full underlying datasets, methods, assumptions, or completed findings.

Report implication: use it in the introduction, study-scope section, stakeholder-process section, and alternatives-analysis setup.

### 2. FAA Airport Environmental Documents, Findings, and Decisions

Source ID: SRC-010

This FAA page is important because it places potential airport actions inside the federal environmental review framework. Any serious Whiteman transformation scenario must account for FAA review, NEPA applicability, environmental documentation, and the level of analysis required for actions with possible significant impacts.

Critical use: cite this source to show that closure, release, redevelopment, or major operational changes cannot be evaluated only through local land-use preferences. Federal airport decisions can require structured environmental review. The limitation is that this is general FAA guidance, not Whiteman-specific.

Report implication: use it in the FAA/legal constraints section, environmental review section, and transition roadmap decision gates.

### 3. FAA Airport Data

Source ID: SRC-011

This is the strongest official baseline source for airport data lookup. It supports the infrastructure side of the case by grounding Whiteman as an active airport in the national aviation data system rather than only a disputed parcel of County-owned land.

Critical use: cite this source for official airport-data methodology or lookup context, then pair it with WHP-specific records, airport master/study materials, or FAA datasets when available. Its limitation is that the page is a lookup portal and does not by itself resolve the full local operational dependency picture.

Report implication: use it in the operational baseline, regional infrastructure, and data/digital twin sections.

### 4. South Coast AQMD Whiteman Airport Lead Monitoring

Source ID: SRC-012

This is one of the most important public-health sources because it directly concerns lead monitoring at Whiteman Airport and nearby communities. It gives the report a technically grounded path into avgas, lead exposure concerns, local air monitoring, and community health risk.

Critical use: cite this source before relying on advocacy or media claims about lead. Its value is technical and location-specific. Its limitation is that air monitoring does not automatically answer every health-outcome question, such as blood lead levels, cumulative exposure, or causation at household scale.

Report implication: use it in the public health, environmental justice, risk matrix, and mitigation alternatives sections.

### 5. OEHHA CalEnviroScreen

Source ID: SRC-013

CalEnviroScreen is the best statewide environmental justice data source for situating Pacoima and nearby communities in a broader cumulative-burden framework. It helps prevent the report from treating airport impacts in isolation from freeway, industrial, socioeconomic, and other pollution-burden factors.

Critical use: cite this source to frame environmental justice conditions and cumulative burden, not to prove airport-specific causation. Its limitation is that it is a screening and comparative tool; it supports prioritization and context but should be paired with local AQMD, health, land-use, and community evidence.

Report implication: use it in the environmental justice section, stakeholder legitimacy analysis, alternatives scoring, and digital twin data-layer concept.

## Inclusion Guidance

- Use Priority A sources in the main report.
- Use Priority B sources for stakeholder positions, background, and narrative context.
- Do not use advocacy or media sources as the sole basis for legal, safety, health, or operational claims.
- Cite the FAA letter, NTSB report, AQMD page, County study site, and County board materials directly wherever possible.
