# FAA and Legal Constraints

Purpose: identify the hard constraints governing closure, release, funding, use, and redevelopment.

Analyze:

- AIP grant assurances
- federally obligated land
- FAA release process
- net benefit to civil aviation
- NPIAS role
- airport revenue restrictions
- compatible land use
- CEQA/NEPA touchpoints
- County authority versus FAA authority

Primary outputs:

- legal constraints memo
- FAA dependency map
- open legal questions
- closure feasibility checklist
