# Public Health and Environmental Justice

Purpose: evaluate community burden and legitimacy concerns as first-order requirements.

Analyze:

- noise
- lead and fuel transition issues
- crash risk perception and actual incident history
- air quality
- cumulative environmental burden
- freeway and rail adjacency
- vulnerable populations
- community trust and process design

Primary outputs:

- burden map
- public health issue memo
- environmental justice requirements
- mitigation options
