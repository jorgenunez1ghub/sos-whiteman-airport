# Mission Decomposition

Purpose: identify the missions Whiteman Airport currently serves and the missions a transformed site could serve.

Analyze:

- general aviation access
- flight training
- reliever airport function
- emergency response
- economic activity
- community health and safety
- housing and land-use opportunity
- environmental justice
- workforce development
- future aviation and smart infrastructure

Primary outputs:

- mission hierarchy
- mission conflict matrix
- measures of effectiveness
- measures of performance
