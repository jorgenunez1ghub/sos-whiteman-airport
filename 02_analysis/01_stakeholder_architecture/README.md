# Stakeholder Architecture

Purpose: map who has authority, who is affected, who can block action, and whose trust is required.

Analyze:

- LA County Board of Supervisors
- Department of Economic Opportunity
- Department of Public Works/Aviation
- FAA
- City of Los Angeles
- Pacoima residents and community groups
- airport tenants, flight schools, pilots, and businesses
- emergency-response users
- housing, labor, development, and environmental justice stakeholders

Primary outputs:

- stakeholder register
- power-interest-risk matrix
- engagement map
- unresolved conflict map
