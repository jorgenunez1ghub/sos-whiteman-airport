# Alternatives Analysis

Purpose: compare feasible future configurations rather than treating the issue as a binary choice.

Candidate alternatives:

- A0: status quo
- A1: airport modernization and mitigation
- A2: aviation retained with community-benefit improvements
- A3: hybrid aviation plus partial non-aviation reuse
- A4: phased transition subject to FAA release
- A5: full closure and redevelopment
- A6: future aviation/smart infrastructure pilot zone

Evaluation dimensions:

- legal feasibility
- safety impact
- community benefit
- aviation network impact
- economic impact
- fiscal cost
- schedule risk
- stakeholder acceptability
- reversibility
