# Assumptions, Decisions, Risks

## Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Status | Validation Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-001 | The study should be framed as a systems transformation problem, not only a land-use dispute. | Working | Validate against County scope, FAA constraints, and stakeholder record. |
| A-002 | FAA obligations materially constrain any closure or repurposing scenario. | Working | Validate through FAA correspondence, AIP grants, and FAA compliance guidance. |
| A-003 | Public health, safety, and environmental justice concerns are central to stakeholder legitimacy. | Working | Validate through County reports, community advisory materials, and public comments. |
| A-004 | Regional aviation and emergency-response dependencies must be modeled before recommending closure or repurposing. | Working | Validate through airport operations data and stakeholder interviews. |

## Decisions

| ID | Date | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-06-09 | Use a numbered, evidence-first folder structure. | Keeps sources, analysis, data, models, and outputs separate as the study grows. |
| D-002 | 2026-06-09 | Treat shared ChatGPT context as Tier 4 input. | Useful for framing, but not a primary evidence source. |

## Risks

| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Analysis overweights advocacy or media claims without primary verification. | Weakens credibility. | Maintain source register and source-tier labels. |
| R-002 | Alternatives are framed as binary closure vs status quo. | Misses feasible hybrid/transition options. | Force multi-alternative analysis. |
| R-003 | FAA obligations are under-modeled. | Produces infeasible recommendations. | Build a dedicated FAA/legal constraints workstream. |
| R-004 | Community impact is treated as secondary to infrastructure optimization. | Reduces legitimacy. | Include public health and environmental justice as a core workstream. |
