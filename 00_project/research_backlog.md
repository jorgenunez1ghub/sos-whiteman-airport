# Research Backlog

## High Priority

- Verify the complete County study scope, schedule, study-team composition, and contract amendment details.
- Build a timeline of board actions, FAA correspondence, study milestones, public meetings, and crash/safety events.
- Map FAA obligations, including AIP grant assurances, land acquisition obligations, release requirements, and the "net benefit to civil aviation" standard.
- Identify all operational dependencies: based aircraft, flight schools, emergency-response uses, reliever function, tower operations, and regional airport network effects.
- Build a source-backed stakeholder register with interests, authority, constraints, and likely objections.

## Medium Priority

- Compare alternatives: maintain airport, modernize airport, hybrid aviation/community reuse, partial redevelopment, full closure/redevelopment.
- Build a risk register that separates safety, legal, political, fiscal, environmental, schedule, and stakeholder risks.
- Define measures of effectiveness and measures of performance for each alternative.
- Identify data needed for a digital twin: operations, noise, emissions, safety incidents, land-use parcels, traffic, environmental burden, economics.
- Build a dashboard concept for decision makers.

## Lower Priority

- Compare Whiteman to Santa Monica Airport, Reid-Hillview, Fullerton, Van Nuys, and other airport land-use conflicts.
- Create a communications plan for public-facing transparency.
- Draft a phased acquisition strategy for studies, pilots, data systems, community engagement, and implementation.
